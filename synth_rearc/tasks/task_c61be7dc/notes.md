`arc2_sonnet45_summary.jsonl` was not reliable for `c61be7dc`: it treated the output bar as a generic compression inside the separator-bounded region, but the official examples show a stricter invariant. The output bar length is exactly the total number of input `5` cells, and the two full `0` separator lines are always redrawn immediately adjacent to that centered bar.

`arc2_opus46_summary.json` was closer on the orientation and line-redrawing pattern, but it still omitted the count-preserving bar length. The implemented verifier and generator therefore use the official examples' stronger rule: detect whether the paired full separators are vertical or horizontal, recover the construction center from those paired lines and the interrupted crossing arms, and count the `5` cells. Then redraw the `5`s as a straight bar of that exact length through the recovered center, with adjacent full `0` lines on either side. The center is deliberately not inferred from either the grid dimensions or the `5` center of mass: translating the entire construction must translate the output, and the official third training shape is asymmetric around its true structural center. In the input, the crossing arms stop at the paired contact surfaces; they do not continue through uncovered cells inside the chamber.
